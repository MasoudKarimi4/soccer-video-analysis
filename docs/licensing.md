# Licensing and external assets

The project is attributed to Masoud Karimi and Zachary Sikka. No explicit license for their original source or report was supplied, so this preparation does not assign a new license to that work. The contributors should select an appropriate repository license if they want to grant reuse permissions.

Dependencies and pretrained models carry their own licenses. In particular, [Ultralytics YOLO11 documentation](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/yolo11/) describes AGPL-3.0 and enterprise licensing. The repository does not include model weights. The other libraries retain their respective upstream terms; pinning a package does not change its license.

The report describes the video as broadcast soccer footage from YouTube, but no exact URL, rights-holder information or redistribution permission was supplied. Full footage and annotated videos are excluded from Git. Selected decoded frames and imported stage illustrations are included as project evidence, with capture/import provenance. Their inclusion does not establish broader redistribution rights or grant an asset license.

The analysis-paper adaptation removes student IDs and repairs image links. It preserves contributor attribution and distinguishes historical claims from fresh verification. The original paper is retained in the user's source bundle.
